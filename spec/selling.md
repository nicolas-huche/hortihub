# Selling

How does a seller manage what they sell?

## Offer

- Product
- Commercial Unit
- Price
- Stock
- Starting Address

## Business Rules

- Only Business who completed the Seller Onboarding can create Offers.
- An Offer must belong to a Product.
- A Seller can create multiple Offers for the same Product.
- Each Offer has one Commercial Unit.
- Each Offer has one Price.
- Each Offer has one Stock.
- A Seller can update their Offers.
- A Seller can deactivate their Offers.
- A Seller can only manage their own Offers.

## Orders Bussiness Rules for Sellers

- Sellers can view Orders containing their Offers.
- Sellers can only see the Order Items associated with their Offers.
- Seller Order Items have their own status.
